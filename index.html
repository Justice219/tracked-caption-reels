Captions that stay glued to the person talking

A Claude Code skill that turns raw talking-head or job-site footage into vertical reels: word stacks that follow the speaker, big key words behind their head, face-tracked crops, tight cuts.

Install in one command
git clone https://github.com/Justice219/tracked-caption-reels ~/.claude/skills/tracked-caption-reels
  1. Paste it in your terminal. It puts the skill where Claude Code looks for skills.
  2. Open Claude Code in the folder with your clips and say: edit these clips in the tracked captions style.
  3. Needs: a Mac with Apple Silicon, Python 3.11+ and ffmpeg. Bring your own fonts, sound effects and music.

What it does

You film. Claude watches every clip, plans the cut from what was actually said, and renders it.

Word stacksEvery line becomes 2 to 4 words in mixed sizes and colours that build as they are spoken.
Behind the headKey words go large behind the speaker, lifted until they stay readable.
Face trackingType rides on the head and chest; jump cuts step through crops that follow the face.
Tight cutsUms, pauses and stumbled lines are cut. B-roll lands on the word it shows.
Lip-sync videosEach take is placed on the exact second of the song it was filmed against.
No boxesNo panels, pills or name plates. Just type, a light grade and quiet music.
