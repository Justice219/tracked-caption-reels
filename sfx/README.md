# Sound effects

The 23 effects the engine draws from (`Picker.POOLS` in `scripts/edit.py`): whooshes for B-roll cuts, soft
ticks as big words land, hits for stamps and stickers, and a payment chime. The engine uses this folder by
default; point `sfx_dir` in `work/project.json` somewhere else to use your own.

Provided by the repository owner, who states they are free of copyright.
